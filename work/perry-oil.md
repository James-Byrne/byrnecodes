## The brief

Perry Oil delivers heating oil, agricultural oil and motor fuel across the south-east. Customers wanted to order online without phoning, and the business wanted prices it could change itself.

## What I built

- **Quick Quote**: pick a county, oil type and quantity and see the price straight away, from price bands the business manages.
- **Online checkout** with card payment, and confirmation emails to the customer and the office.
- **An admin back office** for orders, oil types, delivery zones and pricing.
- **Marketing pages** for home heating, agri oil, commercial customers and budget plans.

## Running it

The site has been in production since 2021. I host and maintain it: dependency updates, payment and email configuration, and browser tests that walk through an order before each release.
